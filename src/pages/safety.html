Track rules & driver conduct

Six rules, one clean race

Simple, clear and covered again in your briefing.

  1. {{icon:check}}

    Follow marshal instructions

    Marshals' calls come first, always.

  2. {{icon:check}}

    Keep control of the kart

    Drive within your limits and keep your inputs smooth.

  3. {{icon:check}}

    Respect other drivers

    Race hard, race clean and give each other room.

  4. {{icon:check}}

    Follow track signals

    When a flag or light changes, act straight away.

  5. {{icon:check}}

    Avoid dangerous contact

    No ramming, no blocking, no brake-testing.

  6. {{icon:check}}

    Follow entry and exit procedures

    Join and leave the track only when instructed.

Flags & signals

Read the track at a glance

Green flag

Track clear. Racing can start or resume.

Go, with care.

Yellow flag

Hazard ahead on or near the track.

Slow down. No overtaking.

Red flag

The session has been stopped.

Slow down and follow the marshals.

Blue flag

A faster kart is approaching from behind.

Hold your line and let it pass.

Black flag

A driver is being called in by the marshals.

Head to the pit lane when shown.

Chequered flag

The session is over.

Finish the lap and follow instructions.

These are common motorsport signals. Your marshals confirm the signals used at the circuit in the briefing.
